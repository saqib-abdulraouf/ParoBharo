📖 Lesson 1/45

Trading kya hai?

Basics se shuru — pehla qadam

Trading ka matlab hai:

Kisi asset ko kam price par khareedna aur zyada price par bechna.

📊 Example:

BTC = $100,000
Tumne Buy kiya.
↓
BTC = $102,000
Tumne Sell kiya.
↓
Profit = $2,000 per BTC (fees alag)
⚠️ Agar price neeche chali jaye aur tum sell kar do, to loss hota hai.

📌 Spot Trading

Spot mein tum coin ke asli owner ban jate ho.

  • Buy BTC → Wallet mein BTC aa jata hai → Baad mein Sell kar dete ho.
✅ Ye beginners ke liye sab se behtar hoti hai.

⚡ Futures Trading

Ismein leverage hota hai.

  • $100 se kabhi $1,000 ki position khol sakte ho (10x leverage).
  • Profit bhi tez hota hai.
  • Loss bhi utna hi tez ho sakta hai.
⚠️ Is liye jab tak Spot achhi tarah na aa jaye, Futures shuru nahi karenge.

📝 Homework

✍️ Aaj sirf ye 3 sawalon ka jawab do:

  1. Spot Trading kya hoti hai?
  2. Futures Trading mein sab se bara risk kya hai?
  3. Buy aur Sell ka matlab apne alfaaz mein likho.

💡 Jab tum jawab de doge, hum Lesson 2 (Binance Interface aur Candlestick) shuru karenge.

🤝 Mera wada

Agar tum roz 30–45 minutes sincerely practice karoge aur homework karoge, to main tumhe step by step is level tak le jaunga ke tum khud charts analyze kar sako aur apni trades plan kar sako.

Main shortcuts ya "100% signal" ka wada nahi karunga, balki woh skills sikhaunga jo lambe arse tak kaam aati hain.
